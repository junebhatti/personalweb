Einstein’s bell curve misses the big kicks in the universe’s first fluid
MIT and UCSB physicists calculated how a heavy quark moving near light speed is jostled by quark-gluon plasma. Large kicks turn out more common than a Gaussian predicts, and a big kick that slows the quark is likelier than an equally big one that speeds it up (Physical Review Letters).
WHY  Einstein’s 1905 Brownian-motion theory balances drag against small random kicks, which works for pollen in water but breaks at relativistic speeds. The lopsided tails carry information about the plasma, suggesting the early universe behaved more like a liquid than a gas.

Today’s question
Why do the memories you can’t summon move you more than the ones you can?
STORY  Lit2Go (USF) · Hans Christian Andersen
The Emperor’s New Clothes ↗
Two swindlers sell a vain emperor cloth that is supposedly invisible to anyone foolish or unfit for his office, then weave nothing on empty looms. Andersen builds a cascade: each official sees nothing, privately doubts himself, and praises the cloth, so every public “yes” becomes evidence for the next person to fake it. It has lasted because, in about 1,700 words, it is the cleanest model of a belief a whole city holds and almost nobody privately believes, the same machinery behind bubbles and prices that drift from what anyone thinks. Read it for how quietly the pressure compounds.
ESSAY  Scent Made Digital · M Dougherty
Uninvited ↗
A scent artist, ambushed in a Brooklyn cab by an ex’s cologne, asks whether a Proustian rush can ever be triggered on purpose. The research she walks through: smell skips the thalamus and reaches the amygdala and hippocampus directly; odor-cued memories in Herz and Schooler’s 2002 study were no more detailed than photo-cued ones but felt far more emotional; and a 2025 EEG study found voluntary and involuntary recall run on distinct processes. Her unsettling guess is that expecting the memory may be what blocks it, and that the effect is blind to whether the memory is happy or painful. It is the lab version of your note on the two kinds of memory.

DESIGN  rauno.me · Rauno Freiberg
Invisible Details of Interaction Design ↗
A Vercel design engineer’s case that interfaces feel human when they borrow physical intuitions (interruptibility, momentum, consistent direction) and when you know what not to animate. Examples: iOS lets lightweight overlays trigger mid-swipe but only dismisses an app when your finger lifts, because dismissal is destructive; he stripped animation from his own keyboard-driven bookmarking tool after it started to feel sluggish at high frequency. These are exactly the details that make a vibe-coded app look vibe-coded. First move: open the app you built that you use most, find the action you do most often, and cut or shorten its animation.
