The world’s most endangered sea turtle nested in Texas 697 times this year, up 55%
The US Fish and Wildlife Service counted 697 Kemp’s ridley nests on Texas beaches in 2026, against 449 in 2025. In 1978 the state saw about one nest every three years.
WHY  A US–Mexico project begun in 1978 moved eggs from Rancho Nuevo to Padre Island so hatchlings would imprint on Texas, raised them in Galveston, and saw the first ones return to nest in 1996. Two seasons are not a trend; partners continue under the binational recovery plan.

CLASSIC  Quotidiana · Seneca
On the Healing Power of the Mind ↗
Seneca, sick again with a chronic illness, writes to his friend Lucilius about how he survived a worse bout as a young man: philosophy and friends kept him going. His argument is that much of suffering is opinion added to sensation, and that replaying past pain makes it bigger than it was. It lasts because it is written from inside the illness, not about it (about 12 minutes). It complicates your note that pain only grows you with room to reflect: Seneca says reflection can also feed the pain.
ESSAY  The Intrinsic Perspective · Erik Hoel
Introducing: Qualiaphobic AI ↗
Hoel proposes building AI that is designed not to be conscious and has a justified belief that it isn’t, so humans stay the only moral patients. The methods: strip models until leading theories of consciousness no longer apply, steer architectures toward things like lookup tables, or fragment any experience they might have. The live objection, raised by his own readers, is that a system doesn’t need an inner life to want things. Good reading before Minds and Machines tonight, and a push on your note that consciousness is private yet the thing we connect through.
ESSAY  henrikkarlsson.xyz · Henrik Karlsson
You learn more about writing from close reading than from writing advice ↗
Karlsson argues that maxims like “show, don’t tell” are scribbled directions to a house, while slow, repeated reading of one great text gives you real tools. His case study is David Foster Wallace’s McCain campaign essay, which spends pages on cameramen and the bus interior and still pulls you forward; he has read the book version four times to see how. The free part stops just before his answer, which is the assignment. Directly useful for your video essays: pick one you love and take it apart.

WORKFLOW  anthropic.com
Building strong safety evaluations ↗
An eval is only as good as what it measures, so borrow psychology’s fixes: decide the question first, then measure the right thing. Their example: refusal rate rewards a model that refuses everything, so they score “appropriate responses” and pair each risky prompt with a harmless look-alike. Grade with a different model, check it against 50 or so transcripts you label yourself, and write your pass thresholds down before you run. All of it transfers to evals for your own agents.
