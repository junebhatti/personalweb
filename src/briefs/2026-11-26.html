US proposes new tariffs on 14 trading partners over factory overcapacity
After an eight-month Section 301 probe, the Trade Representative’s office proposed duties on China, the EU, Japan, India and ten others. China faces 20% on top of first-term tariffs of roughly 25%; EU, Japanese and South Korean goods are capped at the 15% in their trade deals. Mexico and Norway were spared for now.
WHY  The administration is rebuilding the tariffs the Supreme Court struck down in February with a statute that has held up in court; China called overcapacity a false premise, and Japan and the EU rejected the finding. Next: public comment and hearings, with duties unlikely before early next year.

Physicists are using Earth’s magnetic field as a dark matter detector
A Japanese team combing 10 years of data from a magnetometer in Scotland found 65 signals that match what ultralight dark matter should produce. In Leiden, a magnet the size of a dust grain levitates in a vacuum, waiting for something invisible to nudge it.
WHY  If dark matter is made of extremely light particles, it behaves less like billiard balls than like a wave washing through everything, leaving a faint rhythmic trace in magnetic fields. Confirming it needs stations worldwide, because the expected pattern differs by location.

Cognizant in talks to take over S&P Global’s Capital IQ data operations
About 1,500 people in the data and operations team at S&P’s India capability centre would move to Cognizant’s payroll, according to people familiar with the talks; terms are undisclosed and could change. WHY  Handing a labor-heavy operations team to an outsourcer makes a unit lighter before a sale, and Cognizant ran this work before S&P brought it in-house. A textbook business-services lift-out ahead of a likely carve-out.

S&P puts Nvidia’s AA credit rating on watch for a downgrade
S&P Global Ratings placed Nvidia on CreditWatch negative over management and governance risk after Jensen Huang resigned on Nov. 11 and was reinstated by the board on Nov. 22. Any cut is expected to be one notch, within about 90 days. Nvidia fell 3.9% on Wednesday.
WHY  A lower rating raises borrowing costs for a company that sold $25 billion of bonds in June, just as investors question how the AI buildout gets financed. S&P says it will meet the board to discuss remedies.

STORY  celt.ucc.ie · Oscar Wilde
The Nightingale and the Rose ↗
A Student weeps because the girl he loves will dance with him only if he brings a red rose, and his garden has none. A Nightingale who has sung of true lovers every night without meeting one decides he is the real thing, and the rose trees tell her there is a way to make the flower, at a terrible price. Written in 1888 as a fairy tale, it has lasted because its last page turns on the reader; about 2,000 words. Read it against your note asking whether love is something you feel or something you show.
ESSAY  dynomight.substack.com · Dynomight
What’s with all the slide decks? ↗
Decks became the default way to send ideas around not because people got worse at reading, but because consultancies, under the strongest pressure to please clients, drifted from long memos to 200-slide decks once PowerPoint made slides cheap, and the habit spread with every ex-consultant. The counterexample is Amazon, where Bezos banned internal presentations in 2004 and meetings still open with silent reading of a six-page memo. The question for a future banker: what reasoning hides inside a format that is easy to skim and hard to argue with?

REPO  github.com · Anthropic
anthropics/skills: write your rules once, have Claude follow them ↗
A skill is a folder with a SKILL.md file: a name, one line on when to use it, and plain-language instructions that Claude loads only when a task matches. This repo (198k stars) holds a blank template plus the real skills behind Claude’s Word, PDF, PowerPoint and Excel output, so you can read how a production skill is written before writing yours. On paid Claude plans you upload custom skills in settings; in Claude Code it is one command, /plugin marketplace add anthropics/skills. First move: copy the template, write ten lines on how you want problem-set solutions or deal summaries formatted, and upload it.

AI proved the quasi-Riemann hypothesis. Mathematicians are still catching up ↗
OpenAI’s model showed every nontrivial zero of the zeta function lies in a narrower strip than was known, with a 199-page version verified in Lean. Experts say it needed no new concepts, just an overlooked family of functions and a 2021 formula from Dunn and Radziwiłł, yet it is written so badly that Sarnak won’t call it a theorem until humans understand every step.
