Democrats win the House and Senate in a rebuke to Trump

Iran talks stall as Trump floats a “heavy hit”

South Africa’s school HPV programme cut high-risk infections by 83%
Among 2,357 people aged 17–18, HPV 16/18 prevalence fell from about one in five to about three in 100 after the 2014 rollout. Among girls living with HIV the drop was 82%.
WHY  About 87% of eligible girls got a dose in year one, and only vaccine-targeted types fell, which points to the vaccine, not behaviour. Cervical cancer takes decades, so researchers will keep tracking the 2024 single-dose schedule.

New South Wales passes the Great Koala National Park
Parliament approved a 476,000-hectare park on the mid-north coast meant to protect more than 12,000 koalas, including 176,000 hectares from state forest.
WHY  It took more than a decade of campaigning, and funding rests on a carbon-credit scheme that pays to leave public forests unlogged. The Bob Brown Foundation says vital forests were left out and that its campaign continues.

ESSAY  Sour · Priyanka Desai
My AI agent is making me lamer ↗
Agents made Desai more organized and more social offline while her online life turned inward. Instinct, the same agent that texts you, bought her Broadway tickets from a photo of a flyer; she spends more now, because friction was her budget. The “wasted” hours on Discord and Substack were what led to meetups, and she ends unsure which frictions to keep. Feeds your note on agents giving freedom and taking power.
ESSAY  Henrik Karlsson
Four useful questions ↗
Karlsson argues a precise question helps more than advice: why does it feel bad, what are you trying to achieve, what are the alternatives, what would make the biggest difference. When a draft felt off, his wife asked why, and naming the contrived ending gave him the fix. From Richard Hamming he takes a sense of direction, held loosely. Extends your note on mentally pathing toward an outcome.
STORY  marxists.org · Leo Tolstoy
Three Questions ↗
A king decides he could never fail if he knew the right time to act, the right people to heed, and the most important thing to do. He offers a reward, the learned men all disagree, and he goes to a hermit who speaks only with plain folk. About nine minutes long, it has lasted because it turns a management problem into a moral one.

WORKFLOW  Drew Breunig
Context Inertia: When Agents Won’t Let Go ↗
When an agent fixates on a bad hypothesis, more context makes it worse, and compaction doesn’t cure it: dead ends stay in the transcript and get cited again. One Qwen agent debugged package versions for hours when the endpoint was the real bug. The useful habit: when a session circles, reset with a short handoff note instead of arguing with it.
