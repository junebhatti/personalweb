A single gene-editing infusion cut triglycerides by 63% in a trial
Thirty days after one intravenous dose, participants’ triglycerides fell 63% on average, and by up to 85%, Cleveland Clinic researchers reported. No serious treatment-related side effects were observed.
WHY  The edit switches off ANGPTL3, a gene that raises blood fats, so one treatment could stand in for years of daily pills. Next: larger, longer trials to test durability and safety (funded by CRISPR Therapeutics).

A new antibody lowered triglycerides up to 45.5% in a 235-person trial
In a phase 2b trial published in the New England Journal of Medicine, MAR001 cut fasting triglycerides 39.1% to 45.5% versus placebo at 12 weeks, with no more side effects than placebo.
WHY  It blocks ANGPTL4, a protein that slows the enzyme that clears fat from the blood, a different target from story 3. Researchers say the next study tests patients with severely high levels; whether it cuts heart attacks is still unproven.

ESSAY  Substack · Will Etheridge
The Values You’d Write Down Are Probably Not the Values You Live ↗
Everyone runs two value systems: the one they would write on the wall, and the one their calendar, spending and idle hours already reveal. Etheridge, a counselor, says the gap shows up first as a low hum of flatness after hitting goals, and that any value which feels like a quiet obligation was usually absorbed from someone else. His sharpest test: whatever you keep meaning to do and never do, you don’t actually value. It complicates your note on writing down what you believe: the list only works if you also read the one you’re already living.
STORY  Project Gutenberg · Nathaniel Hawthorne
The Haunted Mind (1835) ↗
A short second-person sketch of waking at two in the morning, in the pocket of time where yesterday is gone and tomorrow hasn’t started. Hawthorne’s claim is that in this hour the mind turns into a mirror: it shows every idea vividly but loses the power to choose among them, so Sorrow, Disappointment and Shame walk in uninvited. It has lasted because it names the exact hour when overthinking feels like truth, about eight minutes to read. It feeds your note on mental paths and on being the viewer of your thoughts, not their author.

WORKFLOW  coryzue.com
How I’m writing code in 2026 ↗
Cory Zue, a solo SaaS founder, moved from pair-programming with Claude Code to writing specs and reviewing its branches like a colleague’s pull request. The practical core: turn any task you repeat into a skill, and when the agent drifts, have it edit the skill so the fix sticks; bundle small scripts so the agent gains new abilities. He tried running several agents in parallel and found it wrecked the quality of his review, a useful counterweight to agent-maxxing. His skills are public on GitHub, so you can copy one as a template today.

Polymarket’s US exchange volume is up 266% since June ↗
Monthly volume rose from $4.04 billion in June to $14.77 billion in October, per TickerTracker, which counts trades at full payout value. Combo contracts were 58% of October volume and sports 31%; election markets were only 2.2% of early-November volume despite the midterms. It lands as New York and other states sue over sports contracts and the company seeks $1 billion at a reported $21 billion valuation.
