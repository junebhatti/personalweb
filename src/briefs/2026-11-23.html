COP31 collapses without a deal after two days of overtime
The UN climate talks in Antalya ended Sunday with no overarching agreement, the longest summit on record and the first major breakdown since Copenhagen in 2009. The EU, the UK and small island states rejected a final draft that did not mention fossil fuels.
WHY  The EU says it will not sign text that omits the main driver of warming; Saudi Arabia and Russia blocked fossil-fuel language, NPR reported, and developing countries said the finance offer fell short. Earlier pledges stand; the next round is COP32 in Addis Ababa in 2027.

Voyager 1 is now one light-day from Earth
NASA says the probe crossed the mark on Nov. 18, more than 49 years after launch: its radio signal now takes 24 hours to reach us, so a command and its reply take two days.
WHY  It has coasted on one trajectory at one speed since its 1980 Saturn flyby, so its distance grows like a clock; Voyager 2, on a slower path, will not reach the mark until 2035.

CrowdStrike agrees to buy Zscaler in a $43.9 billion stock deal
Announced this morning with record quarterly results, the all-stock deal gives Zscaler holders 1.135 CrowdStrike shares for each share and values the network-security company at about $43.9 billion.
WHY  CEO George Kurtz says it takes CrowdStrike into the network, security’s largest market; the CFO says it adds to free cash flow per share on day one. Paying in stock days after a tech rout signals a bet that buyers want one platform, not ten tools.

The SEC is examining Nvidia’s accounting, Reuters reports
Two people briefed on the matter told Reuters that SEC enforcement staff are scrutinizing potential accounting issues at the chipmaker; the SEC declined to comment. Nvidia shares fell 14% on Friday.
WHY  Nvidia is the AI buildout’s main supplier and one of its financiers, so questions about its books reach the whole trade. It has no permanent CEO since Jensen Huang resigned Nov. 12; he is due before House and Senate committees Dec. 1.

POEM  poetryfoundation.org · Percy Bysshe Shelley
Ozymandias ↗
A 14-line sonnet, written in 1817 as a friendly contest with another poet, in which a traveller describes a shattered statue of a pharaoh in the desert and the words carved on its base. Its trick is compression: the sculptor’s skill outlives the king it flattered, and the inscription says more than its author meant. It has lasted two centuries because the final lines turn a boast into an image you can’t unsee. Two minutes; read it aloud twice.
ESSAY  jeffxiong.substack.com · Jeff Xiong
The AI Bubble Starts to Burst ↗
Xiong argues the market sold Nvidia’s record quarter because it was pricing the financing behind it, not the earnings. His evidence is the paperwork: by his reading of Nvidia’s filing, receivables rose from $38.5 billion to $81.0 billion in nine months while it holds about $110 billion of stakes in its own customers, and the Fed’s latest stability report flags circular financing. The part a future banker should keep: a comparison to Lucent lending to its customers in 2000, and four questions to ask before signing any AI deal.
ESSAY  aeon.co · Zahaan Bharmal
The physics of getting fired ↗
Fired nine months into consulting in 2001, an Oxford physics graduate asks why hard work did not protect him and answers with chaos theory: economies, like Lorenz’s weather simulations, are deterministic but so sensitive to small shocks that effort and outcome stop lining up. His practical lesson comes from power grids: build surge capacity, slack in time, skills and relationships, because a life run at its limit fails on the first spike. Pairs with your note on making space away from chaos.

REPO  github.com · marimo-team
marimo: a Python notebook with no hidden state ↗
Change one cell and marimo re-runs every cell that depends on it, so your outputs never go stale the way Jupyter’s do; each notebook saves as a plain .py file you can commit, run as a script, or publish as a small web app. For real analysis it makes experiments tactile: plot a sequence, bind ε to a slider, and watch convergence change without writing any UI code. 23.5k stars and active development. First move: run pip install marimo && marimo tutorial intro and finish the intro tutorial.

OpenAI’s agents now finish nearly half of 2–4 hour research tasks ↗
OpenAI’s internal report puts unassisted success on those tasks at 46% in October, up from 23% in March, but jagged: 79% on monitoring training runs, 25% on serving code. Surveyed researchers would give up 40% of team headcount to keep their agents for three months; interviewees say the agents still have poor taste in ideas.
