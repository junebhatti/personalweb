63 countries have now eliminated a neglected tropical disease
WHO says 63 countries had eliminated at least one such disease as of June, up from 42 at the end of 2020. Sleeping sickness cases are down 98% since 1999, and Guinea worm fell to a record low of 10 human cases in 2025.
WHY  The programmes behind the gains, mass treatment, surveillance and vector control, are the ones WHO says aid cuts are now disrupting; resources sit 30% below pre-COVID levels. WHO’s next mark is 100 countries by 2030.

Today’s question
What would you refuse to hand to an agent, even if it did it better?
ESSAY  jxnl.co · Jason Liu
If You Want Taste, You’re Gonna Have to Eat ↗
Once AI makes production cheap, the scarce skill is noticing what is worth making, and Liu defines taste as modeling what people will like, which means risking something other than the average. His method is “eating”: sample widely, transcribe solos, try things on, learn who made what you consume. The catch is that AI removes the friction where this learning used to happen, so he argues for rebuilding it by hand, and treats taste as closer to courage than consensus. Feeds your note on taste and outsourced character.
ESSAY  Substack · Jessica Gwynne
I Have 106 Automations. Am I Missing Something About AI? ↗
Gwynne runs a regulated accounting firm and has built 289 automations in five years; fewer than ten of the 106 still running use AI. She argues the “AI employee” repackages rule-based automation, and that the first question is whether a task should exist at all. She fired her agent, Edna, after it ran live Meta ads that made her look like she wanted a date, yet rebuilt her system in Claude, and that contradiction stays open. Her full framework is paywalled. Feeds your note on agents and lost control over the mundane.
STORY  Standard Ebooks · Anton Chekhov
Misery ↗
Chekhov’s 1886 story follows Iona Potapov, an old sledge-driver in St. Petersburg whose son has just died, through one snowy evening of fares. He keeps trying to tell someone, and each passenger brushes him off. It has lasted because almost nothing happens except a man looking for one listener, in about ten minutes of reading. Feeds your idea of conversation as what keeps you grounded.

WORKFLOW  lucumr.pocoo.org · Armin Ronacher
What is Codemode ↗
Instead of one tool call per turn, the model writes a short JavaScript script that calls tools in a batch, so large outputs stay in the script and out of the transcript. The sandbox has no network or filesystem; tools are the only way out. His example pulls 100 GitHub issues, classifies each in parallel and returns the 12 most frustrated in one call, which would flood context done step by step. He leaves open how well this holds up with smaller models and long-running scripts.

CrowdStrike to shell out nearly $500M for 2 AI security startups ↗
CrowdStrike is buying Fiddler AI (about $400 million), which monitors agents in production, and Dreadnode (about $95 million), which tests models and agents for flaws before launch. The bet is that companies are deploying agents faster than their policies can keep up. Dreadnode was founded in 2023 and had raised $14 million.
