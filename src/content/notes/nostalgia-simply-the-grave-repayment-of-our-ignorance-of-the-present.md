deep thought  
Time is a thief which repays through nostalgia. Nostalgia eats me alive from the core. I constantly fall into traps of reliving every moment I can remember. The best and that worst, the times where I forgot to remember would only happen once. I think I forget to internalize the desolating truth that time is all we have. There's no such thing as the past or the future, just the present. The present is all we will ever know.

This is the reason nostalgia is so gut wrenching. I look back and think of the moments which I was at my highest, where I felt loved and seen. Today, I'm not sure if I feel that way. I often say how company means the world to me, how I care more about who I'm with than what we're doing, but I haven't sought out those relationships recently, and I feel it all coming back to this idea where I feel a severe lack of power in building new relationship from the ground up.

And it's absolutely not that I don't want to, but it's scary, it's hard. I try to be vulnerable, and I know it's important. To be able to share a level a depth that gives others a way to see into your life and relate to you is one of the most important ways to form a long term bond, but I find myself extremely avoidant. But the odd thing is that I know that I enjoy talking and opening, and fostering those insightful, thoughtful, rich conversations, but the hardest part for me is honestly putting myself in a position to have those conversations. It's hard to find the right person, who I can trust, but who also feels a similar way, wanting to connect with strangers.

I yearn for human connection, I think we all do, but we're stuck in this epidemic of suppressing the much needed validation and emotional outlets that we should have. I consume, I rot, I sulk, and it's absolutely not shamelessly. I hate it, but I can't stop it. It's an addiction thats taken me out.

And I think that's why I go through this reoccurring motion of nostalgic waves passing through me. I feel paralyzed as I see the moments I cherished replay in my mind knowing that I'm not really doing anything to create more. I don't feel like I have those sparks and feelings of times in the last few months, I haven't been creating those moments.

Nostalgia is a way that I've been living with the people who I love, who have started to feel like the past. But it's odd, because most of those people are still in my life, but I feel so disconnected to them. And I think this is where I realized how lonely this world really is. You have these raw, intimate moments with the greatest people in your life. You socialize, laugh, cry, and live presently together, but there always comes a point where everything falls apart. You're forced to live your own life, with the only person who you can't escape.

The present moment is me, and it will always be me, and I need to love to be me. But this is arguably the hardest task in life, to be okay with myself, to love myself, and to want to be present with myself. I think a big part of this overconsumption has led to people having a hard time being happy with themselves. It's a classic storyline that social media deteriorates the self-perception, but in reality its absolutely correct for a lot of people. We get so caught up in other people's lives, we not only FORGET to live our own, but begin to become unhappy with it.

I fantasize about all the things I should be doing everyday, no matter how mundane. It's truly a miracle I'm sitting here writing this. I've thought about sitting down and writing probably multiple times a week for the last few months, yet have written less than 10 times. I just laid on my bed and procrastinated for the past 2 hours, waiting for reality to hit.

I just think I really need to discover a way to connect with myself quicker, and to learn how to be in the present, so I can finally live in the only time period I'll ever have, the present.
