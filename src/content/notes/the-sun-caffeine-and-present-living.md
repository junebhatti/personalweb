I'm going to write a recollection of March 24, 25, 26.

March 24 was a Tuesday, the professor's strike was still going on, so I had a lot of asynchronous classes. I started my day late, and thought I was continuing my week on a low note, slow, lazy, and unproductive, but I thought, why not make some plots. I ended up grabbing lunch with Gavin in East Village, we went to this Moroccan/middle eastern style spot called Cafe Mogador, which was excellent. We shared a Turkish coffee and ordered our respective kebab sandwiches. This meal was a lovely start to my day. I followed this up with a Stern study sesh with Mihir, where we were supposed to do Discrete, but he brought two friends, and we ended up not getting much done. Finally, I made my way to intramural basketball, unfortunately but fortunately our game was won because the other team didn't show up. That meant that we ran our own 5v5 with our team and some randoms. It was low energy, but everyone got to get up shots, and I got to break a sweat. It was great to be back on the court with those guys, who I really enjoy spending time with.

March 25 was one of the best days I've had in a while. Urdu got cancelled, so I got my coffee, wrote, and played chess. After about an hour, I went and grabbed another coffee to try a turkish coffee spot with Gavin, turns out I didn't like it. It didn't have milk or sugar, but it tasted wayyy more bitter than the coffee I had at Cafe Mogador, so I definitely need to figure out what about Cafe Mogador was so great. After this I sat down for a quick solo lunch at this spot right under Bobst called Me Chicken Rice, which wasn't great by any means, but I was listening to a Will Ventures podcast with Kushaan Ahuja so that was interesting. Next, with a full stomach, I took a seat on a bench in Washington Square Park, with yet another podcast (Alain de Botton) for a few hours. Now the best part, during this time, I saw a few people I knew who said hi, got interviewed by this lady about restaurants in New York, Ali sat down and chatted with me for 45 minutes, and we got interviewed about ashwaganda by a super chill guy. Then I spent the next 4 hours getting a Bobst session in, where I wrote some more and studied for calc. Finally, I ended the night with a back and bi day, where I saw Sharif. For some reason, maybe everyone's out because of the sun, or I'm finally racking up the amount of people I know, but I keep seeing the few people that I know around, and it's nice to have familiar faces.
