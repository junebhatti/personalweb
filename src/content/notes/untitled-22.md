Two types of memories  
1. The kind that you try to conceive – often failing to do so. The voluntary aspects of it make it so you there's a clear barrier between what you can and can't recall. More of a moment than some bodily experience.

2. The involuntary – evoked from sensory/motor experiences. Forced to be put back into this place. A unique smell transports you back to an exact moment in time; a distinct sight that you remember from many years prior that reminds you of a great story.  
Humans are constructed from their pasts, from those singular moments that accumulate over the time. Those moments that you can't do an active recall on – but show up when you least expect. Much more powerful emotions.
