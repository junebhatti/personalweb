After 6 months, a lot of doubt, and a ton of connections made, I signed my offer to work at Houlihan Lokey in their Business Service's M&A group in the Los Angeles office. This journey has been hard, memorable, and formative. There are a handful of lessons I gathered that I want to share here.

***What I did well***

**Genuine approach**  
First and foremost, I think I took an actual interest in recruiting and the work I would be doing. Initially, in the summer and last year, banking really felt like a buzzword to me. As I began exploration, had smart conversations, and did my own research, I started to realize there are some really interesting deals and strategic positions to take advantage of in the m&a space. I think this came out through my conversations and approach to trends + deal talk, I felt I could ask thought-provoking questions and convey my interest through conversation. I'd say there are a lot of people who approach it for prestige or money, and don't end up doing as well/
